## Known biases

* Survivorship bias: only schemes alive on the download date are included; merged and closed schemes are missing, so category averages are biased upward.
* Daily data only: no intraday prices; realised volatility is built from daily NAV returns.
* Expense ratio timing: TER history covers only the downloaded months; older periods use the latest known TER.
* SEBI 2018 category reshuffle: schemes were re-categorised in 2018, so the current category may not describe a scheme's earlier portfolio.
* No transaction costs, exit loads or taxes are modelled.
* Sectoral/thematic and focused schemes are compared against a broad-market proxy benchmark.

## Findings

* Universe is the open-ended Equity Scheme categories in AMFI NAVAll.txt on the download date, Direct plan, Growth option, with at least 5 years of NAVs.
* Benchmarks are assigned by SEBI category (data/reference/category_benchmarks.csv), not from each scheme's declared benchmark.
